# Lightweight pixel-art materials

This pack contains original, deterministic pixel art generated by [`tools/generate_pixel_art_assets.py`](../../tools/generate_pixel_art_assets.py). The 15 optimized 128 × 128 PNGs are authored on an integer pixel grid without antialiasing. They cover meadow grass and wildflowers, stone paths and promenade paving, four city facades, tree foliage, wood, water, hero suit/cape weaves, and the Hero Town crest.

The active portrait 3D park applies the textures to its ground, paths, waterfront, skyline, trees, furniture, visitors, and Hero Association landmark. The crest also appears in the title screen and hero model. 3D materials explicitly use nearest-neighbor filtering; repeating surfaces tile the source art rather than requiring large images. The complete pack is about 18 KB compressed and decodes to under 1 MB of RGBA texture data, keeping the art practical for low-end mobile devices.

Regenerate the pack with Python and Pillow:

```sh
python3 -m pip install Pillow
python3 tools/generate_pixel_art_assets.py
```
